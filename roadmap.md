## 0. The shape of the problem

Four separate 2.000 GPA gates have to be cleared, not one. Three are nearly met. One is not:

| Gate | GPA credits | Points | GPA | Surplus points needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| University GPA | 100 | 218 | 2.180 | met (+18 cushion) |
| Major/subject GPA | 40 | 75 | 1.875 | 5 |
| Intermediate/Advanced LAS GPA | 60 | 115 | 1.917 | 5 |
| **15 Advanced major credits in residence** | 21 | 27 | 1.286 | **15** |

Each credit earns (grade − 2.0) points: A = +2/cr, AB = +1.5, B = +1, BC = +0.5, C = 0, D = −1.

Retakes do **not** replace grades (confirmed with Heins). The five F's from Fall 2024 stay in every pool
forever. The deficits above are therefore fixed — the only way to close them is new credits at good grades.

Two constraints on top:

- **Spring 2027 term GPA must be ≥ 2.000.** Below that and L&S suspends again at the end of the first
  term back; readmission then requires the deans' permission to even appeal. A bad first term ends the
  December finish.
- **12 credits per term minimum** as an international student. Reduced course load in the final term is
  likely available *(unconfirmed with ISS)*.

The interaction between these two is the thing to watch: **at exactly 12 credits there is no drop safety
valve.** Dropping a class mid-semester puts you under full-time and breaks F-1 status. Taking 13–15
credits buys back the ability to drop one bad class without an ISS emergency.

Leg 0 stops being general self-study and becomes preparation for three specific classes. The mapping is
almost exact:

| Spring course | Prepare with |

| STAT 421 (categorical data) | ISL ch. 4 — logistic regression, classification |

## 3. Parallel tracks

### Chinese — HSK 2.5 → HSK 5 by December 2027

The bottleneck is output, not input. Reading/listening are ~HSK 2.5, speaking ~HSK 1, and sentences are
still being built in English first and translated. Word drills alone won't fix that.

Add, in order of value:
1. **Sentence mining** — collect whole sentences from material you already understand, not isolated words.
   The point is to stop composing in English.
2. **Shadowing** — repeat audio aloud in real time. Fixes tones and rhythm, needs no partner.
3. **A tutor, twice a week** — italki or equivalent, conversation only. This is the one that actually
   moves speaking, and it's cheap enough to survive term time.

Targets (targets, not predictions): HSK 4 on 13 December 2026, HSK 5 in autumn 2027. 
Term time will slow this — plan for 20–30 min/day during terms, more in Phase 0 and summer.

### Life — scheduled, not leftover

- **Guitar**: the course bought ten years ago. Phase 0 is when it gets finished. 20 min/day.
- **Reading**: the 50-book target for 2026 is gone; you're at roughly 8. One book a month is the honest
  number. Circe was the high point — read more of that shelf rather than forcing another *48 Laws*.
- **Cooking**: no target, it's recovery time. Use it as such.
- **Games**: blocked in the schedule like anything else, not as whatever is left at 2am.

The failure mode to watch for isn't laziness, it's avoidance — the thing where the pages become too
unpleasant to open at all. If a week's log goes empty, that's the signal, and the response is to shrink
the task until it's openable again, not to add pressure.
